Just print the following information and do nothing more than that:
- Your current working directory.
- The current AI model being used.
- The current AI model's version.
- The current AI model's capabilities as alphabetically sorted bullet-point-list.
- The skills that are available in the current environment as alphabetically sorted bullet-point-list.
- The mcp-servers that are configured in the environment as alphabetically sorted bullet-point-list.
